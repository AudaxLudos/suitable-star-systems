[hr]
A mod that adds three star systems to explore and conquer.
The first is a perfect star system with planets optimized for colonization.
The second is a remnant star system perfect for farming AI cores and experience.
The last is a [SUPER REDACTED] system perfect for farming unique weapons.
[/center]

[spoiler=Spoilers]
For the omega star system, you need the planet killer and use it on the omega planet to make omega fleets spawn.
The planet killer can be found during the [url=https://starsector.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Scythe_of_Orion]"The Scythe of Orion"[/url] quest or through exploration by discovering a star system with a tundra planet called "Sentinel".
[/spoiler]
[spoiler=Settings]
- Option to not spawn any of the three star systems
- Option to manually place the perfect star system
- Option to skip the [SUPER REDACTED] planet interaction

- If you have lunalib, options are in the mod settings
- If you don't have lunalib, options are in the settings.json file located in "thismodname/data/config/..."
[/spoiler]
